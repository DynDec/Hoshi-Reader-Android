# Privacy Policy

Hoshi Reader Android keeps your books, dictionaries, settings, reading history, and other imported data on your device unless you choose to use a connected feature.

**Google Drive Sync** - If enabled, the app uses the `drive.file` permission to read and write files used with the app in your Google Drive. Synced data can include EPUBs, covers, book titles and authors, reading positions, highlights, statistics, shelves, audiobook playback positions, and Sasayaki match data. Authorization tokens are stored on your device. Your synced files stay in your Google Drive, not on a developer-operated sync server. The optional TTU-compatible sync uses a separate Google authorization connection that you configure.

**Audio** - When you request online word audio, the word or reading is sent to the selected provider. Built-in sources include JapanesePod101, LanguagePod101, and Jisho; you can also add your own sources.

**Anki** - When you export a card, the fields and media you selected are sent to AnkiDroid on your device or to your configured AnkiConnect endpoint.

**Update Checks** - The app requests release information from GitHub and may retry through a proxy if GitHub cannot be reached.

**Third-Party Services** - Google Drive, audio providers, GitHub, and any configured AnkiConnect endpoint handle requests under their own privacy practices. The app does not sell your Google user data or use it for advertising.

**Data Deletion** - Signing out removes locally stored Google authorization credentials but does not delete files in your Google Drive; you can delete those files there. Uninstalling the app removes its app-private local data. Files you exported elsewhere remain until you delete them from those locations.

For questions, open an issue in the [Hoshi Reader Android repository](https://github.com/DynDec/Hoshi-Reader-Android/issues). Please do not post access tokens or private book contents in a public issue.
